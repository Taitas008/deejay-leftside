Deejay Leftside

The Viber.

Deejay Leftside is a Ugandan deejay known as “The Viber,” bringing an energetic, crowd-focused approach to every set.

His sound moves across Afrobeat, Amapiano, Dancehall, Hip-Hop, R&B and Ugandan music, giving each event a flexible soundtrack shaped around the audience.

From parties and clubs to corporate events, weddings, festivals and private celebrations, Leftside’s goal is simple: create a shared moment where the music feels right.
